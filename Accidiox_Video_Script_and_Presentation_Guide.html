3 Word-for-Word Video Script & Visual Storyboard
SCENE 1: The Hook & Problem Reality 0:00 – 0:30
Visual: Quick dramatic cuts of real two-wheeler crashes. Abrupt cut to black with on-screen text: "In a two-wheeler accident, the rider often cannot call for help."
Voiceover (Serious & Authoritative Tone): "Every four minutes, a life is lost on Indian roads — and over 70% of all fatal road victims are two-wheeler riders. When a rider crashes in an isolated area, late at night, or falls unconscious, the biggest killer isn't just the impact — it is the catastrophic delay in emergency medical response. In trauma care, this critical window is known as the Golden Hour."
SCENE 2: The Data & The Golden Hour Gap 0:30 – 0:55
Visual: Graphs showing MoRTH data: 1.7+ Lakh annual fatalities; 44.5% two-wheelers; 50% survival drop after 30 minutes.
Voiceover: "Official data from the Ministry of Road Transport reveals that nearly 50% of crash victims could have survived if emergency services received exact coordinates within the first ten minutes. While luxury four-wheelers come equipped with automated eCall crash systems, 250 million two-wheeler riders in India navigate traffic with zero automated crash detection."
SCENE 3: Introducing Accidiox (The Winning Pitch Line) 0:55 – 1:15
Visual: Studio hero shot of the Accidiox hardware unit with OLED display and LEDs glowing on a clean workbench.
Voiceover (Confident & Inspiring): "To solve this critical challenge, we built Accidiox.

'Accidiox transforms any two-wheeler into an intelligent, connected life-saving vehicle — detecting crashes autonomously, eliminating false alarms through physics-driven filtering, and dispatching multi-channel emergency rescue within seconds.'"
SCENE 4: Full Live Working Demonstration 1:15 – 2:30
Demonstration 4A: Anti-False-Positive Filtering (0:15)
Visual: Vigorously shake the unit and simulate a bump. Show OLED/BLE remains normal. Tilt for 3 seconds then right it up — show no alarm.
Voiceover: "Traditional tilt sensors fail due to potholes, braking vibrations, and speed breakers. Accidiox eliminates false positives using gravity-vector magnitude gating and a 10-second continuous hold filter. If the bike is righted within 10 seconds, the candidate resets completely — zero false alarms."
Demonstration 4B: Sustained Crash & 2-Second Periodic Alert (0:25)
Visual: Keep the unit fallen past 85° for 10s. The hardware buzzer chirps every 2s, hazard LED pulses, and phone triggers the 20s Emergency Window.
Voiceover: "When a genuine fall is held past 10 seconds, the hardware confirms the crash: the onboard buzzer pulses every 2 seconds to alert bystanders without continuous noise fatigue, and broadcasts telemetry to our mobile app. The rider gets a 20-second safety window with a one-tap 'I Am Safe' override."
Demonstration 4C: Autonomous WhatsApp SOS & Database Sync (0:35)
Visual: 20s timer expires. Pop-up appears showing 'WhatsApp SOS Sent'. Switch to receiver phone showing incoming WhatsApp message with live Google Maps link and nearest hospital route.
Voiceover: "If the timer expires, Accidiox autonomously dispatches an emergency SOS via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud API directly to all family contacts — delivering live GPS coordinates, incident timestamp, and the nearest hospital route. Simultaneously, the incident is securely logged to our cloud database and live Incident Map."
SCENE 5: Engineering Architecture & Tech Stack 2:30 – 3:05
Visual: Clean split-screen or animated architecture diagram highlighting the 3 layers.
Voiceover: "Our engineering architecture is built across three production-grade tiers:
1. Hardware Layer: ESP32 dual-core SoC paired with an MPU-6050 6-DOF IMU, running real-time Euler angle fusion and low-latency BLE GATT.
2. Frontend Layer: A modern Progressive Web App (PWA) utilizing the Web Bluetooth API for live bi-directional telemetry streaming and Web Audio synthesis.
3. Cloud & Telematics Layer: Hosted backend leveraging the Meta WhatsApp Graph API (v20.0), Geoapify Places API for automated hospital geocoding, and a MySQL relational database for audit-proof incident tracking."
SCENE 6: Future Scope & Real-World Scaling 3:05 – 3:45
Visual: Renders/mockups showing Accidiox integrated into electric scooters and centralized ERSS emergency dispatch dashboards.
Voiceover (Strong Conclusion): "Looking ahead, Accidiox is architected for mass commercialization:
• OEM Integration: Seamless embedded integration into EV and ICE two-wheeler CAN bus and OBD-II systems.
• National 112 ERSS Dispatch: Direct integration with India's Emergency Response Support System for automated ambulance routing.
• Insurance Telematics: Tamper-proof crash blackbox logs for Usage-Based Insurance (UBI) and accident reconstruction.

With Accidiox, we are ensuring that no rider on Indian roads is ever left alone in the Golden Hour. Thank you!"
